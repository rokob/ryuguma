[*Cloud Cuckoo Land*][book-amaz] is a weird title and I don't really know why I decided to pick it
up but I am very glad I did. I am writing this over a year after reading it as I am catching up on
writing these. It was a page turner in the best sense of that term. I was drawn in and held until
the last moment. A bit too in your face about this idea of the power of books but I really enjoyed
the intertwined stories across such disparate characters over great spans of time.

[book-amaz]:      https://www.amazon.com/Cloud-Cuckoo-Land-Anthony-Doerr-ebook/dp/B08TRMSR3Z/
