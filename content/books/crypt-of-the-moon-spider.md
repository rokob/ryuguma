I picked up this book at *Shakespeare and Company* in Paris around Halloween on a shelf for
recommendations from the staff for scary books to read. This thing was extremely short and
absolutely batshit. I am feeling that it was good, but it was so surreal that I don't really even
know how I feel about it. It was short enough that I'd say you should just read it if you are open
to something wild. I wouldn't particularly call it scary but I don't feel that way about most books.
