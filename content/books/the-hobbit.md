[*The Hobbit*][book-amaz] is a book I have clearly known about for a long time but never got around
to reading. I finally decided to read it and follow with the Lord of The Rings.

I have not seen any of the movies or read any of these books. I mentioned that to someone and they
were surprised I had managed to avoid them this long. I know the general gist of them as they are
part of the zeitgeist but nonetheless the vague notions of a thing and the real thing are quite
different.

I really enjoyed this book. It was different than I was expecting. I think I was expecting something
much more epic and instead it was quite contained and thoroughly readable. It made me excited to
read the rest of the books.
