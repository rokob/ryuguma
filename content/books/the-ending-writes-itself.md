I was in a bookstore and saw, [*The Ending Writes Itself*][book-amaz], on a shelf as a staff pick. I
read the blurb and decided to write the title down to maybe pick it up later. Well this was the time
and I am glad I did. Another mystery thriller in the style of Agatha Christie who I absolutely love.
Now don't get me wrong this wasn't on that level but it was quite an enjoyable read. Clearly it was
written by writers who have had some annoying time with the writing establishment but this came
across as a niche critique with less appeal to a general reader. It was cute and the ending was not
as surprising as a Christie novel.

[book-amaz]:      https://www.amazon.com/dp/0008739277
