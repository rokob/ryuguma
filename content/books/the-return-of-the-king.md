Finally the end, [*The Return Of The King*][book-amaz]. It took longer than the others for me to
work my way through. More detail and a bit more action to follow. You can't not read this one and
you can't read it without reading the others, so again it sort of is just a continuation of a great
set of books. I would definitely recommend this and I don't know why I put it off for so long.
