*The Road* is a famous book at this point. I had heard of the title but I had no idea what it was
about or why it was well known. About halfway through the book I realized I was probably not going
to get any closure.

The writing is interesting. At times it is quite emotional through the pacing and word choice. But
at other times it is cryptic and the esoteric vocabulary is more of a hinderance than anything. I
enjoyed it, but it is not something I would call truly great.
