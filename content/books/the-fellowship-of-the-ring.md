After *The Hobbit* I knew that I was going to read the Lord of The Rings triology so I quickly
got to [*The Fellowship Of The Ring*][book-amaz] and it did not disappoint at all. Truly I
understand why people are so into this story and the world. The writing is good, the story is
engaging, and although there is a lot of detail I didnt find it to be superfluous. I am definitely
going to plow through them all.

[book-amaz]:      https://www.amazon.com/Lord-Rings-One-J-R-R-Tolkien-ebook/dp/B007978OY6/
