It took me forever to read this book not because it was not amazing but mostly from my own scattered
attention and other interests. It was also quite dense at times and required me to sit and think
about it so I wasn't turning the pages as quickly as I wanted to. [*The Wind-Up Bird Chronicle*][book-amaz]
is the first Murakami book I have read. The world is fantastical and I have heard his books are
gentle and jazz-infused. The writing is amazing or maybe I should say the translation. I never know
how much the original and the translation are to be recognized when the writing is so good. I have
to believe it is the original especially since Murakami is actively participating in the translation
process.

The whole thing I could never explain. Someone asked me what I was reading, then what it was about,
and I didn't even know how to start. I said it was about a man and his wife and things happen. That
was the best I could do without getting into the details. I am really interested in reading more of
his books after this.
