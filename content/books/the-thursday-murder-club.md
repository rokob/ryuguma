I love a good murder mystery and [*The Thursday Murder Club*][book-amaz] is exactly in my preferred
style of the genre. Funny, thrilling, good writing. There were definitely moments where the writing
was dragging a bit, and I wouldn't call it masterful in the suspense but it was great nonetheless.
