I bought *You Dreamed of Empires* for Kudu when she was going to Mexico City as I had heard a review
of it and thought it would be  fitting book for the trip. She said I should read it and it has been
a couple years but I finally got to it.

Strange, difficult to pronounce, and very good. It was a very interesting conceit for a story and it
was executed quite well. The writing is wonderful. I wouldn't put it in the great category as it
felt disjointed at times and I don't know why. Something like it didn't fully pull me in. The book
did convey the sense of a drug induced stupor quite well.

I definitely recommend this one. It was pretty short. Reading all the names was a challenge for me.
